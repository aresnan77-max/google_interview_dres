# Self-Guided Behavioral Mock Interview — 30 Minutes

## Setup
1. Set a timer for 30 minutes
2. Record yourself (phone/webcam) — review afterward
3. Answer aloud as if speaking to a Google interviewer
4. 3 minutes per question maximum

---

## Question Bank (Answer 8-10 Questions)

### Round 1: DevRel Core (10 min — pick 3)
1. Why do you want to work in Developer Relations at Google?
2. Tell me about a time you helped a developer overcome a technical challenge.
3. How do you stay current with developer tools and technologies?
4. Describe content you created that had measurable developer impact.

### Round 2: Collaboration & Communication (10 min — pick 3)
5. Tell me about a time you worked with a team that had conflicting priorities.
6. Describe a time you had to explain a complex technical concept to a non-technical audience.
7. How have you handled negative feedback from a developer community?
8. Tell me about a cross-functional project you contributed to.

### Round 3: Problem Solving & Growth (10 min — pick 3)
9. Describe a time you failed and what you learned.
10. Tell me about a decision you made with incomplete information.
11. How have you dealt with a significant technical disagreement?
12. What's the most creative solution you've come up with for a developer problem?

---

## Answer Framework (STAR — 2 minutes each)

For each question:
- **S** (15 sec): Set the scene quickly
- **T** (15 sec): Your specific responsibility
- **A** (60 sec): Detailed actions YOU took
- **R** (30 sec): Quantified results + what you learned

---

## Self-Assessment (After Recording)

Watch your recording and rate:

| Criterion | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | Score |
|-----------|---|---|---|---|-------|
| **Clarity** — Was your answer easy to follow? | Rambling | Somewhat clear | Clear | Crystal clear | /4 |
| **Specificity** — Did you use concrete examples? | Generic | Some detail | Specific | Vivid + metrics | /4 |
| **Ownership** — Did you say "I" vs "we"? | Always "we" | Mix | Mostly "I" | "I" with team credit | /4 |
| **Structure** — Did you follow STAR? | No structure | Partial | Good STAR | Perfect STAR | /4 |
| **Authenticity** — Did you sound genuine? | Scripted | Somewhat real | Genuine | Passionate + humble | /4 |
| **Conciseness** — Under 3 min per answer? | >5 min | 3-5 min | 2-3 min | ~2 min perfect | /4 |

**Score**: ___ / 24

- 20-24: Interview-ready
- 15-19: Good, polish weaker areas
- Below 15: Practice more, focus on STAR structure

---

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ **Talking too long** — practice 2-minute answers, not 5-minute stories  
❌ **Being too humble** — own your impact, interviewers want to see YOUR contribution  
❌ **Forgetting the Result** — always end with a measurable outcome  
❌ **Generic answers** — "I worked hard and it went well" tells nothing  
❌ **Negative tone** — even failure stories should end with growth  
❌ **Not preparing enough stories** — have 10 stories mapped to multiple questions
