## Google Evaluation Criteria

| Pillar | What They Look For |
|--------|-------------------|
| **Coding** | Correct + optimal solution, clean readable code, complexity analysis |
| **System Design** | Structured requirements, scalable architecture, trade-off reasoning |
| **Behavioral** | STAR format, developer empathy, collaboration, handling ambiguity |
| **Communication** | Clear articulation, teaching ability, technical depth + breadth |
